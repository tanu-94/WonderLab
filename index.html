What is CT & AI in Class 3?

A simple, clear guide for teachers and parents on why we build mental models before code[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).

THE HUMAN HABIT

1. Computational Thinking (CT)

What it is: A step-by-step problem-solving method. The logical process of breaking down a large task, spotting repeating patterns, and organizing clear instructions[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).

What is the use in daily life?
  • In School: Solving word problems, grouping numbers into hundreds and tens, and organizing tiffin boxes[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).
  • At Home: Packing school bags according to the timetable, or following a recipe step-by-step[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).
THE MACHINE APPLICATION

2. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

What it is: Technology that mimics human decision-making by recognizing patterns from past examples (data) to make predictions or choices[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).

What is the use in daily life?
  • Voice Recognition: Recognizing voices when asking a phone or speaker to play music.
  • Data Privacy & Security: Protecting parents' UPI payments, ATM PINs, and passwords using secret keys[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).
